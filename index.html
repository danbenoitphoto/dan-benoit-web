Questions

Answered straight.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your site needs — a simple bilingual site is a very different job from a property launch with drone video and renovation previews. That's why there's no price list here: we talk through what you need, shape the level to your budget, and you get the full price in writing before anything starts. No surprises, and nothing billed you don't need.

Can't I just use a template builder?

You can — many do. You get the same layout as thousands of other businesses, stock photos, one language unless you translate it yourself, and your evenings spent fighting the editor. A custom build is designed around your business, photographed by a pro, bilingual from day one, and looked after for you. The slider at the top of this page shows the difference better than words can.

How long does a build take?

10–14 days from our first call, once I have what I need from you. The clock pauses if we're waiting on content or feedback — your launch moves, it doesn't get rushed.

Is the French version a real translation?

Yes — every page is written in French as its own version, not run through a widget. In Prescott-Russell your French-speaking clients can tell the difference immediately.

What happens when I need changes later?

You send them to me. New photos, new hours, new listings, a new page — weekly or monthly updates are done for you, and hosting, backups and a monthly report are part of the care. You never log into a builder.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. If you ever move on, you keep everything — I'll hand over the files and explain your options in plain language.
