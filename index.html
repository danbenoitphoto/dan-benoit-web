Questions

Asked by local businesses, answered straight

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your site needs — a simple bilingual site is a very different job from a property launch with drone video. That's why there's no price list on this page: we talk through what you need, shape a package to fit your budget, and you get the full price in writing before anything starts. No surprises, and no paying for features you don't need.

I already have a website. Why rebuild it?

If yours is slow on phones, only in one language, hard to update, or doesn't show your current work, it's quietly costing you calls. A rebuild keeps your domain and your Google presence, replaces the dated design, and puts updates on a care plan so it stops going stale. If your current site is genuinely fine, I'll tell you — the 15-minute call is free either way.

I have a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?

Facebook is rented ground: people have to be on Facebook, logged in, and lucky enough to be shown your post. Your website is yours — it shows up on Google and in AI search answers, works for everyone, and puts your services, photos and phone number in one place that you control. Most clients keep their Facebook page and let the website do the heavy lifting.

How long does a build take?

10–14 days from deposit and a completed intake form. The clock pauses if we're waiting on you for content, photos access or feedback — your launch moves, it doesn't get rushed. I build a maximum of two sites a month.

Is the French version a real translation?

Yes. Every page is written in French as its own version — not a Google Translate button. In Prescott-Russell, your French-speaking clients can tell the difference immediately.

What do you need from me?

Surprisingly little: your logo if you have one, your services and prices, any photos you already have, and access to your domain account (I'll walk you through it). A short intake form and one 15-minute call cover most of it. If writing isn't your thing, copywriting from your bullet points is an add-on.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and on full payment the site files are yours. If you ever cancel a care plan, you keep your site — I'll hand over the files and explain your options in plain language.

What does ongoing care cover — and not cover?

It covers hosting management and site changes (text, photos, prices, hours, listings) done for you weekly or monthly, whenever you need them — plus a monthly plain-language report and backups. It doesn't cover redesigns, new sections, advertising or social media posting — those are quoted separately first, never added to your bill as a surprise.

Can you guarantee I'll rank #1 on Google or get more leads?

No — and be careful with anyone who does. What I guarantee is the work: a fast bilingual site, real photos, correct search setup, and honest monthly numbers so you can see exactly what your site is doing. That's what gives a local business its best shot, and you'll be able to verify it yourself every month.
