Questions

Answered straight.

What makes a website truly bilingual?

Two complete versions of the site, one in English and one in French, each with its own pages, its own search titles and descriptions, and text written for that audience. The versions are linked together properly so Google shows the right language to the right person. A translate button or a plugin is not the same thing.

Is the French version just a translation of the English?

No. Each version is written as its own text, with the phrasing, tone and details that sound natural in that language. A sentence that works in English often needs a different shape in French, and your French-speaking clients can tell when nobody bothered.

Why does bilingual matter so much in Prescott-Russell?

A large share of your customers live, shop and search in French, and many expect to be served in French by local businesses and institutions. A site that only speaks English quietly sends those customers to a competitor who bothered. A bilingual site keeps both halves of your market.

What happens when I need a change to my site?

You send the change once, in whichever language you prefer, and both versions are updated together so they never drift apart. Hosting, backups and a plain-language monthly report are part of the care, and you never log into a builder.

Will both versions show up on Google?

Yes. Each version has its own address and its own titles and descriptions, and the two are connected with the language tags search engines expect. Someone searching in French finds your French pages, and someone searching in English finds your English pages.
