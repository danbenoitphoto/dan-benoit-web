Why hire a web designer in Embrun instead of an Ottawa agency?

Because you deal with one person who lives and works where your customers do. There is no account manager, no ticket queue and no handoff to a junior team. Dan designs, builds, photographs and maintains your site himself, answers his own phone, and knows the Embrun and Prescott-Russell market because it is his market too.

How long does a website take to build?

Most sites launch 10 to 14 days after our first call, once I have your content and feedback. If we are waiting on photos, text or approvals, the launch moves with you rather than getting rushed. You review a private preview link before anything goes live.

Will my website be in French as well?

Yes. Every site comes with a complete French version written as its own text, not a machine translation of the English. In Embrun and across Prescott-Russell, your French-speaking customers notice the difference right away, and Google indexes both versions.

Can you photograph my business for the website?

Yes. Dan is a professional photographer, and a photo session of your business, team or products is available as its own service alongside the website. If you already have good photos, they can be placed and edited professionally instead. Either way, your site shows your real business, never stock photos.

What happens after my site launches?

Hosting, security and backups are looked after, and when you need a change you send it over and it gets done for you. You also receive a plain-language monthly report on visitors, calls and inquiries. You never have to log into a website builder.
